Veriyo

South Africa's Repair Price Transparency Platform

Know what a repair should really cost — before you pay for it.

Car repair pricing in South Africa is rarely upfront. Veriyo makes it visible — real prices from real drivers, so you know what's fair before you agree to anything.

Why Veriyo Exists

Most drivers only find out what a repair "should" cost after they've already paid for it. Workshops set prices with little visibility into what others charge, and have no easy way to prove they're being fair to a new customer.

Veriyo exists to change that, by putting real prices — reported by real people — out in the open. Every driver who shares what they paid makes the picture clearer for the next person. Every workshop that publishes its pricing gives customers a reason to trust it before they ever walk through the door.
